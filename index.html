06 / Answers

Questions

What is an agent?
Software that does a job someone on your team does today. It follows the same steps, sources and rules. It runs every day and shows its work.
Does it replace my team?
No. It does the research, checks and first drafts. Your people take the calls and close.
What does it work with?
Your CRM, email, Slack and the data you already pay for.
How long until it's running?
A first agent usually runs on sample accounts within two weeks. Cold email needs 4 to 6 weeks for new inboxes to warm up.

Tell us which job eats the most hours. We'll show you the agent for it.
